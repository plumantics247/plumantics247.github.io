# The Simstrology Family: A Player's Guide

*Working draft, October 23, 2026. Covers the full Core family; release-specific installation details are being checked before publication.*

Simstrology gives your Sims a birth chart, a changing sky, and new ways to build stories around both. You can keep it as a layer of personality and everyday atmosphere, or expand it into childhood identity, a subject to study, a life goal, a university major, and a profession.

Start with **Simstrology Core**. Every addition in this guide builds on that foundation, but you can choose the parts that suit your save. A Sim does not need a university education to have a chart, and your whole household does not need to become professional Simstrologers. Someone still has to fix the sink.

## 1. Start with Core: your Sim's chart and the world's sky

**PlumAntics Simstrology Mod**, called **Core** throughout this guide, is the foundation of the family. It is playable on its own.

Core connects two things:

- **Your Sim's natal chart:** the chart identity established for that particular Sim.
- **The current sky:** the moving planetary placements shared across the save.

That distinction matters. Your Sim's natal Sun is part of their identity. The Sun's current position belongs to the world around them. Optional systems can use the changing sky without replacing who your Sim is.

### Meet the Big Three

The **Big Three** are Sun, Moon, and Rising. They describe different parts of the same Sim.

| Placement | What it means for your story | Why it matters in the system |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Sun** | Core identity and self-expression. | Establishes the Sim's Sun-sign identity. |
| **Moon** | Emotional needs and inner reactions. | Establishes Moon-sign identity and gives Lunar Moods something personal to respond to. |
| **Rising** | First impressions and the way a Sim meets the world. | Anchors the house layout and identifies the chart ruler. |

You can begin with a Sun-and-Moon story. Add Rising when you want a fuller chart. Some additions, including Planetary Pressures, require all three placements before their gameplay begins.

### Give a Sim their chart

For human teen-and-older Sims, the **Simstrology Hub** is the starting point for chart setup, chart and sky information, settings, and Cheats and Repairs.

There are three ways into chart setup:

| Setup route | What you do | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Sun First** | Start with a chosen or universe-chosen Sun, then work through Moon and Rising. | Building a particular character, or discovering their identity one placement at a time. |
| **Rising First** | Start with a chosen or randomized Rising and let the broader chart fill in from the current sky. | Starting from first impressions and a house-based chart. |
| **Find Natal Chart From Current Sky** | Capture a chart from one moment of the live sky for an eligible Sim without an existing Big Three. | Letting the current Simstrological moment establish the Sim's chart. |

The current-sky option sets up the selected Sim. It does not automatically give the entire household matching charts.

These setup routes lead into the same connected system. They are choices about how you begin your Sim's story.

### What Core contributes to every addition

Core keeps the save's Simstrological sky consistent and provides the chart foundation used by the rest of the family. That lets a Sim's identity, the current sky, and an installed addition refer to the same world.

For the player, the payoff is continuity: the chart you establish can become part of a childhood story, a subject your Sim learns to interpret, or the foundation of their professional work.

### Core requirements

The current full Core requires **The Sims 4 Seasons**, **XML Injector**, **Lot 51 Core Library**, **Lumpinou's Mood Pack**, and **Zerbu's Custom Preferences**.

Install the required Core and strings packages together with Core's runtime `.ts4script`. Enable both **Custom Content and Mods** and **Script Mods Allowed** in the game settings.

Core can show a missing-requirements notice after loading a household, provided its own script is running. After installing or enabling missing requirements, fully restart the game.

## 2. Shape everyday play with Core's optional features

These features are optional packages associated with the Core download. They expand different parts of the experience; they are separate choices from the companion mods described later.

### Horoscopes: a small prompt for today's story

**Adds:** personalized, sign-based horoscope readings.

**Why add it to Core?** A chart gives your Sim an identity; a horoscope gives you a quick way to check in with that identity during play. It is an approachable starting point if you enjoy astrology flavor without wanting to interpret every placement.

Use a reading as a storytelling prompt for the day. It gives you something to respond to without requiring a long study or career path.

### Lunar Moods: the same Moon, different feelings

**Adds:** short-lived mood buffs shaped by the current moon phase and your Sim's Moon sign.

**Why add it to Core?** It makes the Moon part of everyday life. Two Sims under the same moon phase can respond differently because their natal Moon signs differ.

New, waxing, full, and waning phases can give different emotional flavor. The result is a gentle nudge toward a mood or an activity that fits it, giving players more material for household storytelling.

### Retrogrades: cosmic timing meets ordinary chores

**Adds:** retrograde moodlets and small effects following eligible completed actions.

**Why add it to Core?** It connects the moving sky to things Sims already do. A retrograde becomes something you may notice while repairing, socializing, studying, or getting through the day.

| Retrograde | What it can add to ordinary play |
| --- | --- |
| **Mercury** | Occasional extra wear after eligible object use. |
| **Venus** | More complicated low-relationship connections, with a small boost to some relationship-repair actions. |
| **Mars** | Extra tiredness after eligible strenuous activity or repairs. |
| **Jupiter** | Small rewards for mentoring, tutoring, and rereading skill material. |
| **Saturn** | Small completion rewards for responsibilities such as homework, bills, cleaning, or repairs. |

These effects depend on the relevant retrograde and action. They do not guarantee that something will happen every time. When an effect applies, an in-game notice helps explain it.

### Sign Compatibility: more chart flavor between Sims

**Adds:** chart-based preferences and compatibility flavor involving Sun, Moon, and Rising.

**Why add it to Core?** It brings Simstrology into how Sims relate to each other. Their charts become another source of ease, interest, or friction for the player to explore.

Choose it for friendship and relationship storytelling. A compatibility pattern gives you material for a relationship story; it does not promise a particular relationship outcome.

This package changes preference-related systems. Check the release's specific requirements and compatibility guidance, especially if you use other mods that alter CAS likes, dislikes, or custom preference categories.

### Houses & Progressions: where life happens, and what changes with age

**Adds:** house context and age-based Progressed Sun gameplay.

**Why add it to Core?** It gives a chart more detail in two useful directions: the life area involved, and the chapter your Sim is currently living through.

**Houses** describe life areas. The twelve houses cover identity, possessions, communication, home, romance, health, partnerships, intimacy, philosophy, reputation, community, and spirituality. Rising anchors their layout.

A house adds context to a placement: you can ask where a theme is showing up in your Sim's life, as well as what that theme means.

The **Progressed Sun** describes a developmental chapter associated with age. It sits alongside the natal Sun. Your Sim keeps their natal identity while gaining another layer for their current story.

**Pair it with Simstrology Skill for deeper interpretation.** Level 3 supports awareness of active transit context. Level 4 opens calculation and interpretation of house placements and the current Sun Progression. Installing an interpretation feature and having a Sim skilled enough to use it are separate steps.

This combination suits players who enjoy tracking a Sim's changing priorities over a long save.

### Outer Planets: a broader chart

**Adds:** Uranus, Neptune, Pluto, and Chiron chart and transit content.

**Why add it to Core?** It expands the range of placements available to a player who wants a more detailed Simstrological picture.

Choose it when you enjoy deeper chart exploration. Its content is separate from Planetary Pressures: installing Outer Planets does not add Uranus, Neptune, Pluto, or Chiron pressure moodlets.

## 3. Add more life-stage or emotional depth

These are separate companion downloads that build on Core without requiring your Sim to follow an academic or professional path.

### Simstrology Childhood: start the story before the teen years

**Adds:** childhood Sun, Moon, and Rising identity for infants, toddlers, and children, with child-stage traits, mood and personality flavor, and a transition into teen-and-up chart gameplay.

**Why add it to Core?** Core's main onboarding begins at teen age. Childhood gives younger Sims their own earlier chapter, so Simstrology can follow a family across more of its life.

The childhood versions are age-appropriate chart identity. At teen age-up, the childhood state hands over to the teen-and-up system.

**Best for:** family saves, legacies, and players who want a Sim's early identity to feel connected to their later life.

**Try this story:** follow siblings from childhood into their teens, using their different chart identities as prompts for how they respond to the same household events.

### Planetary Pressures: give an existing feeling a cosmic echo

**Adds:** at most one visible **+1 moodlet** reinforcing an emotion your Sim already has.

**Requires:** Core **2.7.1 or newer**, Lumpinou's Mood Pack, the Planetary Pressures gameplay and strings packages, and its runtime `.ts4script`. The Sim must be teen or older with a complete **Sun, Moon, and Rising**. There is **no Simstrology skill requirement**.

**Why add it to Core?** It makes the shared sky emotionally noticeable during normal play, without adding a study requirement.

For example, an already Angry Sim can receive a matching Mars-in-Aries pressure. That describes **Mars in the current sky**, rather than a requirement for an Aries natal placement.

Pressures support the seven traditional planets: Sun, Moon, Mercury, Venus, Mars, Jupiter, and Saturn. A supported placement and a matching emotion must both be present. A Sim can have a complete chart and still receive no pressure that day.

If several planets qualify, one pressure takes priority: **Moon, Mercury, Venus, Sun, Mars, Jupiter, then Saturn**. Pressures do not stack hidden emotional weight. When the other moodlets supporting that emotion disappear, the pressure releases too.

**How it differs from Lunar Moods:** Lunar Moods responds to moon phase and natal Moon sign. Planetary Pressures responds to supported current planetary placements and an existing emotion. They offer different ways for the sky to color everyday play.

**Installation note:** do not install the legacy Core `PlanetaryConditions` package alongside Planetary Pressures; they share Mars buff resources.

## 4. Add Simstrology Skill: turn a chart into something to learn

**Adds:** a five-level Simstrology skill for teen-and-older Sims, with research, reading, theories, and study activities.

**Why add it to Core?** Your Sim can move from having a chart to developing the knowledge to interpret it. It gives you active things to do and a reason to return to Simstrology over time.

Study options include computer research, reading different Simstrological traditions, themed theory interactions, and class or lecture content where supported by your installed setup.

Books offer different perspectives, including **Evolutionary**, **Hellenistic**, **Modern**, and **Vedic** Simstrology. That gives you room to play a reflective seeker, a traditional scholar, or an enthusiast who has acquired several very strong opinions.

### The levels that connect the family

| Skill milestone | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| **Beginning study** | Gives the Sim a learning path through research, reading, and practice. |
| **Level 3** | Supports active transit awareness with the relevant Core feature; qualifies a Sim for the career's licensing-exam route. |
| **Level 4** | Opens deeper house and Sun Progression interpretation with Houses & Progressions. |
| **Level 5** | Completes the skill track and supports later professional growth. |

Skill also supplies progression and interactions referenced by Aspirations, Degree, and Career. For the full learning-and-life-path experience, build from **Core + Skill**, then choose the goal, university, or work content you want.

## 5. Add Astrological Aspirations: give the story a direction

**Adds:** multi-stage **Karmic Soul Paths**, with themed goals and a completion reward.

**Build on:** Core + Simstrology Skill.

**Why add it to Core?** A chart can suggest a personality; an aspiration gives you something to pursue with that personality. The paths turn growth themes into milestones involving social life, emotions, skills, and everyday actions.

The four paths in the current source are:

| Karmic Soul Path | A storytelling lens |
| --- | --- |
| **South Node Aries / North Node Libra** | Explore independence, cooperation, and partnership. |
| **South Node Cancer / North Node Capricorn** | Explore comfort, responsibility, and ambition. |
| **South Node Aquarius / North Node Leo** | Explore belonging, individuality, and creative visibility. |
| **South Node Virgo / North Node Pisces** | Explore control, trust, and meaning. |

These lenses describe the stories you can tell with the paths; the aspiration panel supplies the actual objectives.

Completing a path grants **Soul Path Master**, a permanent reward trait centered on social poise. Successful social moments can trigger a brief Confident upswing.

**Best for:** players who like a goal panel to guide a long-running character arc. You can pursue these stories without sending your Sim to university or into the Simstrologist career.

## 6. Add the Simstrology Degree: make it an education

**Adds:** a Simstrology university major, themed coursework, study and extra-credit support, graduation rewards, and diploma support.

**Build on:** Core + Skill + **Discover University**. Add Career for the professional continuation described below.

**Why add it to Core?** It gives Simstrology a place in a university save. Your Sim can commit to formal study, manage a term, and leave with a qualification connected to the wider family.

The course themes include zodiac signs, planets, houses, aspects, mythology, interpretation, statistics, sky mechanics, and timing. The degree uses the familiar university rhythm of classes, homework, papers, exams, and presentations.

### What graduation adds

Graduation brings degree recognition, a Simstrology diploma, and the hidden **Formally Trained Simstrologer** perk.

The degree also connects to professional entry. The current career setup recognizes a completed Simstrology degree as an alternative to the licensing exam, with higher entry positions for graduates:

| Qualification | Simstrologist career entry |
| --- | --- |
| **Regular Simstrology graduate** | Level 2, Chart Reader. |
| **Honors Simstrology graduate** | Level 3, Consultation Analyst. |

Regular graduation provides a Simstrology skill floor of 3; honors graduation provides a floor of 4. A floor raises a lower skill to that point rather than taking levels away from an already more skilled Sim.

**Best for:** university players who want their Sim's subject, study activities, and future profession to belong to the same story.

## 7. Add the Simstrologist Career: turn knowledge into work

**Adds:** a professional career with shared early progression and three later specialties.

**Build on:** Core + Skill. A Simstrology degree supplies an alternate entry route.

**Why add it to Core?** It gives a knowledgeable Sim a professional future. Chart interpretation becomes part of working life, with clients, public communication, and a choice of professional identity.

### Getting into the profession

Career entry is for young adults, adults, and elders. There are two routes:

1. Reach **Simstrology level 3**, pass the **licensing exam**, and join the career.
2. Complete a **Simstrology degree**, which provides an alternative to the license requirement.

The exam belongs before career entry. You do not need to max the five-level skill before beginning professional practice.

### Build a practice, then choose a specialty

The shared early ladder runs through **Associate Simstrologer**, **Chart Reader**, **Consultation Analyst**, and **Simstrologer**. The branch choice comes at level 5.

| Specialty | What kind of Sim it suits | What it brings to play |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Private Practice** | A one-on-one chart reader. | Client scheduling, natal-house readings, progression reviews, and client fees. |
| **Public Forecasting** | A communicator who wants an audience. | Blogging, forecast videos, interviews, and Sim TV content. The in-game title **Mundane Simstrologer** refers to this public forecasting role. |
| **Transformational Guidance** | A reflective, meaning-focused practitioner. | Guidance sessions and interpretation of natal themes and progressions, with an emphasis on personal growth. |

Private Practice and Transformational Guidance share a client-reading foundation but frame it differently. Private Practice emphasizes explaining the chart; Transformational Guidance emphasizes the meaning a Sim can take from it.

**Best for:** players who want Simstrology to remain useful after skill mastery or graduation, with a professional direction that fits their Sim.

## 8. Choose a combination for your save

You can add depth in several directions. These are suggested combinations, rather than a requirement to install the whole family.

| The experience you want | A useful combination | What you gain |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **A chart and occasional story prompts** | Core + Horoscopes | An accessible introduction to chart identity and readings. |
| **A sky you notice during ordinary play** | Core + Lunar Moods + Retrogrades; add Planetary Pressures with its requirements | Moon-based atmosphere, action-based timing effects, and reinforcement of existing emotions. |
| **A family story that starts young** | Core + Childhood | Early chart identity and continuity into the teen years. |
| **A self-taught chart enthusiast** | Core + Skill + Houses & Progressions | Study that opens deeper interpretation of the Sim's chart and current chapter. |
| **A guided personal-growth story** | Core + Skill + Aspirations | Concrete milestones and a long-term reward. |
| **A university-to-profession story** | Core + Skill + Degree + Career + Discover University | Formal study, graduation recognition, and a professional continuation. |
| **A professional without university** | Core + Skill + Career | A licensing-exam route into the profession. |

Childhood, Planetary Pressures, and the Core feature packages can sit alongside the learning and career paths. They do not require your Sim to become a scholar or professional to have a place in the household's story.

## 9. Your first session

1. Install Core and its requirements, then choose the optional features and companion mods that fit your save.
2. Open the Simstrology Hub for a human teen-or-older Sim and choose a chart setup route.
3. Complete Sun, Moon, and Rising if you want to use features that need the full Big Three.
4. Begin with one clear activity: read a horoscope, start researching Simstrology, select a Karmic Soul Path, or simply play and observe the household.
5. Return to the Hub for chart information, settings, and repairs as needed.

A chart can stay a small part of a Sim's personality or grow into their life's work. Choose the additions that give you more of the stories you enjoy playing.
